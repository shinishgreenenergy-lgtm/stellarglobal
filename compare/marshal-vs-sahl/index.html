Marshal vs Sahl

What Marshal does on the dimensions buyers weigh when replacing a GRC platform. We state our own position and point you at Sahl's documentation rather than characterising their product for them.

This page states Marshal’s own position only. We do not characterise Sahl’s product second-hand.

Questions worth asking on each dimension, and where Marshal stands.
DimensionMarshalWhat to confirm with Sahl
Saudi regulatory frameworksNCA ECC, NCA CSCC, SAMA CSF, SAMA BCM and PDPL ship as first-class control sets.Ask whether these are native control sets or a custom framework you have to build and maintain yourself.
Regulator reference IDsEvidence is filed against the clause IDs the regulator uses, so submissions need no translation sheet.Ask to see an exported evidence pack and check whether the clause IDs match the regulator's own numbering.
Cross-framework mappingOne satisfied control maps to every framework clause it also satisfies, automatically.Ask how many times the same evidence has to be attached when you carry several frameworks.
LanguageArabic and English, including the exported audit pack.Ask whether Arabic covers the full product and its exports, or only the interface.
Access modelRead-only, scoped per system. No agent runs on your workloads and nothing is written back to production.Ask what write scopes the connectors request and whether an agent is installed.
ImplementationDelivered by the Stellar Global team, including migration with dates preserved and a parallel run.Ask who performs the implementation, and what it costs beyond the licence.
