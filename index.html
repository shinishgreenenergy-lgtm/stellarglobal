Anatomy of an automation

Connect once. It never stops running.

01

Connect

Read-only OAuth or a scoped role per system. No agents in production, no write scopes, no exceptions.

02

Test

The automation runs its check on its own cadence — hourly, daily, on an HR event, on a merge.

03

File

The result lands in the control record with a timestamp, the raw response and the framework clauses it clears.

04

Escalate

A failure opens a task on the named owner, posts to their channel, and ages against the remediation SLA.
